Trench Arcane - Alpha Playtest Sheet

Target Game Duration: 20 Minutes (Approx. 8-12 Turns)

Playtest Objectives (What to watch for)

The 20-Minute Timer: Does the game consistently end before the Deck Depletion (Supply Line Attrition) rule kills a player? If games are ending via Deck Depletion too often, health pools are too high or damage is too low.

Snowballing: If Player A successfully Pillages early and gets an Ephemeral resource advantage, can Player B recover, or does the game run away immediately?

Stalling: Does the Demolitionist keyword effectively counter the Razor-Wire Redoubt? Does the Sapper effectively counter the Resonance Mortar?

DECK A: THE IRONCLAD SIEGE (Metal / Aether)

Playstyle: Heavy control, massive armor, and artillery standoffs. Wants to test the "Stalling" limits of the game.
Command Center: High Command: Ironclad Bastion (0/3/40. Buildings cost 1 less Metal).
Resource Lineup: 4 Metal, 2 Aether.

The 40-Card Decklist (Print 4 of each):

[Unit] 4x Trench-Forged Fusilier (Cost: 2M | Stats: 2/0/2 | Suppressive Fire)

[Unit] 4x Diesel Dreadnought (Cost: 6M | Stats: 3/1/5 | Move immediately on deploy)

[Unit] 4x Aether-Lens Sniper (Cost: 2A | Stats: 2/0/1 | Piercing)

[Unit] 4x Resonance Mortar (Cost: 5A | Stats: 4/0/3 | Artillery. Generates 1 Ephemeral Aether on kill)

[Unit] 4x Aetheric Colossus (NEW!) (Cost: 11A | Stats: 6/0/8 | Piercing. Suppressive Fire)

[Building] 4x Razor-Wire Redoubt (Cost: 2M | Stats: 0/2/4 | Deals 1 damage to advancing enemies)

[Command] 4x Hasty Entrenchment (Cost: 2M | +1 Armor to target until end of turn)

[Replacement Command] 4x Scrap-Metal Shrapnel (NEW!) (Cost: 1M | Deal 1 damage to an attacking unit. If that unit has 0 Armor, deal 2 damage instead.)

Token & Tracking Requirements

To run this playtest smoothly, you will need:

Resource Tracking: 12 six-sided dice (one for each of the 12 Resource cards on the board to track banks from 0 to 3).

Damage Tracking: A handful of tokens or a spindown D20 for each player's Command Center, and smaller tokens for Unit damage.

Biomass Tokens: At least 6 blank cards or proxies to represent the Graft-Fiend 1/0/1 tokens generated by Deck B.

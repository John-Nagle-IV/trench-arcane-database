Trench Arcane - Master Card List

Playtest Set v2.0 (Full 3-Copy Legal Set)

Stat Notation: Attack / Armor / Health

COMMAND CENTERS (Limit 1 per deck, starts on board)

1. High Command: The Grand Redoubt

Stats: 1 / 2 / 35

Ability: Trench Logistics — The first Unit you deploy each turn costs 1 less resource of any type.

2. High Command: Ironclad Bastion

Stats: 0 / 3 / 40

Ability: Deep Entrenchment — Buildings you deploy cost 1 less Metal (to a minimum of 1).

3. High Command: The Charnel Vats

Stats: 2 / 0 / 35

Ability: Flesh is Cheap — During Pre-Volley Deployment, you may take 2 unpreventable damage to your Command Center to deploy a 1/0/1 Graft-Fiend unit token into your Base.

4. High Command: Resonance Spire

Stats: 2 / 1 / 30

Ability: Arcane Overcharge — Once per turn, spend 1 Ephemeral Aether to give a target friendly unit Piercing until end of turn.

5. High Command: The Sapper's Warren

Stats: 1 / 2 / 35

Ability: Spoils of War — Whenever a unit you control successfully Pillages an enemy Resource, heal 1 Health to this Command Center.

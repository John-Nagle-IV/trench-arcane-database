Trench Arcane - Command Centers (Initial Archetypes)

Command Centers serve as the player's proxy on the battlefield. They dictate the deck's overarching strategy and provide a thematic anchor for the 40-80 card deck.

Baseline Stats: 35 Health | 2 Armor | 1 Attack

1. High Command: The Grand Redoubt

The Standard Issue (Balanced / Midrange)

Stats: 1 Attack | 35 Health | 2 Armor

Ability: Trench Logistics — The first Unit you deploy each turn costs 1 less resource of any type.

Flavor: "Hold the line. Reinforcements will be there when the mud dries." — General Radek

Playstyle: Highly flexible. Excellent for mixed-resource decks looking for a steady, reliable tempo advantage every single turn.

2. High Command: Ironclad Bastion

The Metal Turtle (Defensive / Artillery)

Stats: 0 Attack | 40 Health | 3 Armor

Ability: Deep Entrenchment — Buildings you deploy cost 1 less Metal (to a minimum of 1).

Flavor: "Let them break their teeth on our steel. We have enough shells to bury whatever is left."

Playstyle: Stalls the game. The monstrous 3 Armor makes it immune to anything but heavy tanks and massive swarms. It wants to build Razor-Wire Redoubts and Artillery to bleed the enemy dry.

3. High Command: The Charnel Vats

The Biomass Swarm (Aggro / Token)

Stats: 2 Attack | 35 Health | 0 Armor

Ability: Flesh is Cheap — During your Pre-Volley Deployment, you may choose to take 2 unpreventable damage to your Command Center. If you do, deploy a 1 Attack / 1 Health Graft-Fiend Unit token into your Base.

Flavor: "Do not mourn the fallen. Bring me their parts; the war effort demands recycling." — Stitch-Doc Vane

Playstyle: Highly aggressive. 0 Armor means it takes every point of damage thrown at it, but its 2 Attack kills most infantry that touch it. The player sacrifices their own health to flood the board with cheap units and overwhelm the enemy early.

4. High Command: Resonance Spire

The Aether Spire (Control / Magic)

Stats: 2 Attack | 30 Health | 1 Armor

Ability: Arcane Overcharge — Once per turn, you may spend 1 Ephemeral Aether to give a target friendly unit Piercing until the end of the turn.

Flavor: "The crystals sing a beautiful, terrible song. Cover your ears and fire the cannon."

Playstyle: Wants to play Aether cards and pillage. By granting Piercing, it allows even weak units to sunder heavy armor, completely countering the Ironclad Bastion archetype.

5. High Command: The Sapper's Warren

The Underground Network (Pillage / Sabotage)

Stats: 1 Attack | 35 Health | 2 Armor

Ability: Spoils of War — Whenever a unit you control successfully Pillages an enemy Resource, heal 1 Health to this Command Center.

Flavor: "Why build our own bullets when the enemy is kind enough to keep them in boxes across the field?"

Playstyle: Promotes highly interactive, surgical strikes. Wants to use Swift units and Sappers to constantly raid the enemy's resources, sustaining its own health in a long war of attrition.Trench Arcane - Command Centers (Initial Archetypes)
